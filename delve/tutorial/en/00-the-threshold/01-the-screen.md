# What You're Looking At

The Porter watches you walk the last few steps toward him and seems satisfied by
something.

"There. You've already learned the hard part, and nobody had to tell you. You wanted to
be over here, so you came over here."

**That's movement.** The arrow keys: up, down, left, right. You've been doing it for ten
seconds.

"The rest is just knowing where to look. Four parts."

**The top line is the message line.** It tells you what just happened. When something
matters, it appears there, and only there.

**The middle is the dungeon.** You're the `@`. The lines are walls, floors are `.`,
corridors are `#`. What you can see is what you've been near; the black is simply where
you haven't gone yet.

**The second line from the bottom is you:**

```
Ada the Novice   Dlvl:0  Rooms:1/4  $:0  HP:12(12)  T:14
```

`Dlvl` is how deep you are; this floor is 0, because you haven't gone anywhere yet.
`Rooms` is how much of this floor you've finished, so the size of what's ahead is never a
mystery. `HP` matters downstairs, and Alwin will explain why. `T` counts turns and does
not judge you.

**And the last line tells you which keys do something right now.** It changes as you go:
it says one thing while you're walking and another while I'm talking. If you forget which
key you need, don't try to remember it. Look down.

> 👀 Message line on top. Dungeon in the middle. You along the bottom, and the keys under
> that. That's the whole screen.

Some floors have things lying about worth picking up, coins among them. You never have to
remember the key: when you're standing on something you can take, or carrying something you can
set down, the bottom line tells you how. Look down.

The Porter nods at the far wall, which has no door in it.

"One question and I'll let you through. It's an easy one. I'm not here to catch you out;
none of us are, whatever the last training you sat made you expect."
