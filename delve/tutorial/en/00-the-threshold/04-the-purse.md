# What Gold Is For

Merryn counts a small stack of coins into a purse and slides it across the stone toward you,
then thinks better of it and rests a hand on top.

"Not yet. First you answer three questions about what the others already told you, in your own
words. Pass, and these hundred coins are yours. Fail, and we try again; nothing here can hurt
you."

**Downstairs, gold buys a helpline.** On a multiple-choice or yes-or-no question, you can spend
coins to strike out a wrong answer. The score still counts; you are paying in coin, not in
marks. That is different from asking the jackal, which costs you a point on the room. Use the
purse when you want help and want to keep the marks.

"Up here the helpline is closed," Merryn adds. "Orientation does not take your money. Take the
coins with you for the floors that do."

> 💰 A hundred coins for a clean sitting. Downstairs they buy a helpline that removes a wrong
> answer; the score stays yours.

"Three questions. Message line, pockets, doors. Then the stairs."

## Questions

### Where on the screen does the game tell you what just happened?

- ?answer: message line, the message line, top line, the top line, along the top, at the top, top of the screen, the top of the screen
- ?reject: status line, bottom line, the map, a popup

> The message line, along the top. It is the one place news appears. The bottom lines are who
> and where you are, and which keys work right now; they do not announce what just changed.

### How do you pick up something that is not a coin, and how do you put it down again?

- ?answer: comma, the comma, press comma, pick up, pickup, drop it, press d, the d key, put it down, put it back down
- ?reject: kick it, throw it, eat it, walk over it

> Stand on it and press `,` to pick it up; press `d` to put something down. Coins collect
> themselves when you step on them; everything else waits for the comma.

### You run out of HP and wake at the start of the floor. What happened to the doors you had already earned?

- ?answer: still open, stay open, remain open, never lose, doors stay, progress stays, nothing lost, still earned, still unlocked, they're still open
- ?reject: reseal, close again, start over, lose progress

> They stay open. Running out of HP puts you back at the floor's entrance and nothing else;
> every door you earned stays earned. Progress is never taken away for finding something hard.
