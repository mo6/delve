# The Feeling

A phishing email works by rushing you. It wants you moving before you think, because thinking is
what kills the attack. Everything else in the message is set dressing around that one push.

## Questions

### In one word, name the feeling a phishing email manufactures to stop you thinking.

- ?answer: urgency, time pressure, being rushed, panic
- ?reject: fear of the boss, curiosity, greed

> Urgency is the lever. The message wants you moving before you check the one thing that would give
> it away. With a model installed, a paraphrase like "it makes you feel rushed" is understood; the
> offline keyword floor wants one of the listed words instead.
