# The Sender

A mail from a colleague's real, correct address is not automatically safe. A correct address proves
the mail came from that account, not that your colleague sent it.

## Questions

### A correct sender address only proves the mail came from that account. In a word or two, what is the case where every address check still passes?

- ?answer: account takeover, compromised account, the account was hacked, account compromise
- ?reject: spam, a typo in the domain

> Account takeover is exactly the case where every address check passes, which is why "verify the
> sender" cannot be the whole habit. The judgement moves to the content and the context.
