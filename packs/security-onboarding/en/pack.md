# The Caverns of Compliance

Somewhere below this building there is a room with your name on a door, and a
keeper waiting behind it who would like a word about your password.

Four floors. Twelve keepers. None of them will let you past on charm.

You have done this training before, somewhere else, as a slideshow with a progress
bar that you clicked through while eating lunch. This one is a dungeon. The doors
are real, in the sense that they will not open, and the only way out is down.

Bring your wits. Leave your certainty at the stairs.
