# Thank you, thank you very much

Rachel has a bag. Inside the bag is everything Phoebe did not think to prepare.
She has been maid of honor before. She has opinions. She has a sexy voice she
only breaks out when Ross walks in and the job is on the line.

"Hi, Ross. Sweetie," she coos, then drops it the second he is useful. "Look,
Phoebe is lovely, and also improvising. I brought materials. I brought a
speech. I brought the energy of someone who has done this at least twice and
still remembers Barry and Mindy's wedding."

When the coin flip goes to Phoebe, Rachel hands over the bag like a graceful
loser. When Phoebe hands the job back, Rachel does a full Elvis: "Thank you,
thank you very much." Joy lasts until Monica mentions the meetings.

> Over-preparing is how Rachel competes when she cannot bear to look like she
> is competing.

"I am fine. I am great. I am going to need those meeting times in writing."
