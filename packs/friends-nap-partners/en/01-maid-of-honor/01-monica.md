# I can't decide, so you decide, then I decide everything

Monica is already holding a list. The list has sub-lists. The sub-lists have
meeting times.

"I love you both," she says. "Equally. Which is why I cannot choose a maid of
honor, and which is why *you* have to choose, and which is why, once you have
chosen, we will be having regular meetings about flowers, seating, and the
exact second the rings appear."

She taps the clipboard. "People think the hard part is picking between Rachel
and Phoebe. Wrong. The hard part is that I already know how this wedding goes.
I just need a maid of honor who can survive my binder."

"Also: if I ever get fat, Chandler had better not do the camp-girlfriend thing.
We will circle back to that on the other floor. Stay focused. Maid of honor.
Now."

> Asking your friends to choose for you does not mean you stop having opinions.
> It means the opinions arrive later, with a schedule.

"Flip a coin if you must. Then clear your calendar."
