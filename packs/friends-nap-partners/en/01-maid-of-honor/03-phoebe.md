# Want a little taste of Pheebs?

Phoebe is scoring well. Joey has invented maid-of-honor auditions. Ross is
playing the drunk uncle. Phoebe's solution to a yelling relative mid-ceremony
is not a whisper. It is a scream and a tackle.

"Your honor?" she says, already committed. "I am not here to blend into the
centerpieces. I am here to solve the problem. Drunk uncle? Horizontal. Next
hypothetical."

She wins the coin flip, sees Rachel's bag of effort, and gives the job away
because that is the kind thing. Then Rachel points out the twist: if Rachel is
Monica's maid of honor, Monica will be Phoebe's. Phoebe's face does a whole
arc in one second.

> Being generous with a title does not delete the fine print. Read the rotation
> before you donate the bouquet.

"This is hilarious when it is happening to Rachel. It is less hilarious now."
