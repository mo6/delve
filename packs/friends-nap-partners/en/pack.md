# The One Beneath the Apartment

Somewhere under Monica and Rachel's place there is a dungeon that smells like
coffee, warm milk, and a photo album nobody should open before brunch.

Two floors. Six keepers. One maid of honor. One camp girlfriend. One nap that
was definitely weird and also kind of nice.

You have seen this episode before: Phoebe and Rachel fight over a bouquet,
Chandler tries to say "fat" without saying "fat", and Ross and Joey fall asleep
on the same couch. This is that episode as a dungeon. Each friend gets one
room. The doors stay shut until you can quote them properly.

How you doin'? Leave your certainty at the purple door.
