# Ten is the highest

Ross is defending two positions at once: that the scoring scale makes sense,
and that he is definitely not going upstairs to nap with Joey.

"Why is ten the highest?" Joey asks. "Because it's the highest," Ross says,
with the full academic weight of a man who has corrected museum tour guides.
He plays the drunk uncle for the audition. He notices Rachel's cologne
offensive and decides it is every bit as good as Giorgio. He refuses the second
nap because it is weird. He takes the second nap because it was also, against
his better judgment, great.

Then the whole gang walks in. The noise they make is basically "Hmm."

> You can footnote the awkwardness and still show up for the couch. Just own
> the scale: ten is the highest.

"Why would I care where you are napping? No reason. I am coming anyway."
