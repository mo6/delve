# Fa-aa-aw-ow

Chandler is holding a photo album like it might explode. Monica has already
said the word he would not say.

"I did not say fat," he insists. "I said fa-aa-aw-ow. Completely different
syllable. Very scientific. Julie Graff came back to camp the third summer and
had gotten really… that. So I ended it. Which, yes, makes me sound like a
terrible person from summer camp, which is a very specific genre of terrible."

Monica makes him apologize. Julie, it turns out, did not even know why he
dumped her. So now he has explained it out loud to the one person who was
happily not thinking about it. He returns to Monica and tries a joke about
elephants never forgetting. She is not amused.

> Sarcasm cannot unsay a shallow reason. If you dumped someone for looking
> different, say the apology without the encore.

"Seriously. Good luck marrying me."
