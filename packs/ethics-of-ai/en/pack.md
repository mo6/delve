# The Halls of Judgement

Somewhere under the lecture theatre is a corridor of sealed doors, each kept by
someone who will not accept "the algorithm decided" as an answer.

Seven floors. Twenty keepers. The questions are older than the machines.

You have read about AI ethics before, as principles on a slide. This is a dungeon.
The doors stay shut until you can say what the principles cost.

Bring your judgement. Leave the checklist at the stairs.

Inspired by the University of Helsinki course *Ethics of AI*
(https://ethics-of-ai.mooc.fi/). Lessons here are rewritten for Delve; they are not
a copy of the MOOC text.
