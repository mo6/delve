# What is your quest?

The Bridgekeeper asks three questions. The gorge does the rest. Lancelot
passes on easy ones. Robin dies of overconfidence. Galahad dies of indecision.
Arthur asks a clarifying question and watches the Bridgekeeper fall in.

"The trial looks like trivia," says the Bridgekeeper. "It is a test of
calibration. Know what you know. Admit what you do not. Do not perform
certainty you have not earned. And when the question is underspecified, ask."

"Robin treats difficulty as an insult to his ego. Galahad treats a simple
question as a puzzle worthy of committee. Arthur does the one adult thing left
in the script: he demands the missing fork."

> Match your confidence to your information. Clarifying questions are how you
> stay on the bridge.

"Name, quest, favourite colour. Then the swallow. You know the rest."
