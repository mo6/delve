# The Caverns of Camelot

Somewhere under England there is a dungeon that smells faintly of coconut, and
of a very small rock that someone insisted could float.

Seven floors. Seventeen keepers. A witch in the mud, a knight who will not
admit defeat, Frenchmen with a cow, and a rabbit that is not cute.

You have seen this quest before, as a film with a killer rabbit and a lot of
mud. This one is a dungeon, ordered as the film runs: coconuts first, Bridge
of Death last. The doors stay shut until you can say *why* the crowd was wrong,
*when* a scratch stops being a scratch, and *which* swallow they meant.

Bring your scepticism. Leave your certainty at the stairs. And if anyone offers
you a shrubbery, take notes.

Scenes drawn from *Monty Python and the Holy Grail* (1975); lessons rewritten
for Delve.
