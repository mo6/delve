# There are some who call me… Tim

Tim stands on a rock and throws fire around because that is how you establish
credentials when your name is Tim.

"You expected Merlin," he says. "You got Tim. The juxtaposition is the joke,
and also the warning: expertise does not always arrive in the costume you
ordered. I know where the cave is. I know what guards it. Whether you listen
depends on whether you can hear useful information through a silly name."

"Knights love grandeur. Grandeur is how they walk into deaths that were
clearly labelled. My job in this room is to make you take a ridiculous-looking
briefing seriously when the content is specific."

> Discount the costume last, not first. A precise warning from someone named
> Tim is still a precise warning.

"Follow the smoke. Not the branding."
