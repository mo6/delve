# That's no ordinary rabbit

Caerbannog is small, white, and responsible for several knights. Tim said so.
The knights charged anyway.

"Look at the bones," Caerbannog says politely. "Then look at me. People trust
their first glance more than a warning that contradicts it. Cute is a hell of
a disguise. So is anything that fails to match the monster template you
brought with you."

"The Holy Hand Grenade is the punchline and the procedure: when the threat is
real and your instincts are wrong, you need a method, not another speech about
how it 'couldn't be'. Brother Maynard even has instructions. Instructions are
what you reach for after denial finishes killing people."

> Appearance is not risk. If a credible source says the cute thing is lethal,
> update before you charge.

"Also: one… two… five. Three, sir. Three."
