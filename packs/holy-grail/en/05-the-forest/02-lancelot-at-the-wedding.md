# A rescue, sort of

Lancelot holds a note that arrived by arrow. It speaks of marriage, misery,
and a need for rescue. He does what brave knights do: he runs, for a very long
time, and then kills rather a lot of wedding guests.

"I inferred a maiden," he says, not quite apologising. "The author was a
prince who did not want to marry. The inference was heroic and wrong. Bravery
without reading carefully is just velocity."

"The film gives you the joke twice: first the endless run toward the castle,
then the discovery that the damsel narrative was a bad parse of a short
message. I am not against rescue. I am against inventing the victim in the
missing lines."

> Do not flesh out a brief with the story you were hoping to star in. Check
> who wrote it and what they asked for.

"Afterward I say sorry. That helps socially. It does not un-storm the wedding."
