# We are the Knights Who Say Ni!

Ni stands under the trees and says the word again, because that is the job.

"Ni! We are the keepers of arbitrary conditions," Ni explains. "First we demand
a shrubbery. Then another shrubbery. Then we ban a word, and the ban matters
more than whether you found the shrubbery. The point of us is not horticulture.
It is watching what people will do when the rules are nonsense but the fear is
real."

"Arthur tries to comply. That is rational when the alternative is Ni. It is
also how you end up in an infinite errand. Later, the weakness is that we
cannot stand the word *it*. Arbitrary power often has an arbitrary soft spot.
The lesson is not 'find a magic word'. The lesson is: notice when demands keep
moving, and ask what would actually count as done."

> When the requirements change every time you meet them, you are not failing
> the quest. The quest is failing you.

"Also: ni. That is all."
