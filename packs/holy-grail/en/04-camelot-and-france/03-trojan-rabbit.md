# The Trojan Rabbit

Bedevere stands beside an enormous wooden rabbit and looks, briefly, proud.

"The Greeks had a horse," he says. "We have a rabbit. The principle is sound:
hide inside the gift, wait, open the gates from within. The failure mode is
also sound, and we hit it perfectly. Nobody got in."

He knocks on the hollow flank. "A plan has a payload. Ours was 'knights
inside'. We built the container and forgot the contents. Then the French
catapulted the empty rabbit back at us, which is what you do with a threat that
never armed itself."

> A clever shape is not a plan. If the critical step is missing, you have built
> a prop.

"Write the steps. Name who is inside the rabbit. Check before you push it to
the gate. That is the whole lesson, and also why we are covered in splinters."
